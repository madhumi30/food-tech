Food Concentration

Concise notes prepared from the main concepts identified in your uploaded study materials. Use the question bank for exam practice.

Definition and purpose

Concentration removes part of the water from a liquid food and increases the proportion of solids.

Evaporator types

Open-kettle evaporation is simple; flash evaporation is rapid; thin-film evaporation gives short heating time; vacuum evaporation lowers boiling temperature.

Freeze concentration

Freeze concentration forms ice crystals that are separated from the concentrated liquid, helping limit heat exposure.

Membrane separation

Ultrafiltration retains many macromolecules such as proteins, while reverse osmosis separates water from dissolved substances using a dense membrane and pressure.

Applications

Applications include fruit juice concentration, dairy processing and reducing volume before drying.
