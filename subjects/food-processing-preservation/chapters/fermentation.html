Fermentation and Microbes

Concise notes prepared from the main concepts identified in your uploaded study materials. Use the question bank for exam practice.

Microbial groups

Fermentation transforms food components through microorganisms or their enzymes. Major groups include bacteria, yeasts and moulds.

Lactic acid bacteria

Lactic acid bacteria produce lactic acid, lower pH and contribute to preservation and flavour.

Biochemical changes

Fermentation may produce acids, alcohols, gases, flavour compounds and changes in texture.

Benefits

Potential benefits include improved flavour, digestibility and reduced levels of some antinutritional factors.

Control and safety

Temperature, salt, time, hygiene and raw-material quality affect fermentation. Poor control may cause spoilage or safety risks.
