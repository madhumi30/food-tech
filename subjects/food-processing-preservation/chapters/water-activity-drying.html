Water Activity and Drying

Concise notes prepared from the main concepts identified in your uploaded study materials. Use the question bank for exam practice.

Water activity

Water activity is the ratio of water-vapour pressure above food to that above pure water at the same temperature: aw = p/p⁰. ERH (%) is approximately aw × 100.

Free and bound water

Moisture content measures total water; water activity indicates its availability. Free water is more available than bound water.

Drying purposes

Drying removes moisture to lower water activity, slow microbial growth and reduce storage and transport costs.

Moisture movement

Moisture movement mechanisms include capillary movement, solid shrinkage, solid diffusion and surface diffusion.

Factors affecting drying

Drying rate depends on temperature, relative humidity, air velocity, food thickness and structure, and dryer design.
