Traditional Meat Processing

Concise notes prepared from the main concepts identified in your uploaded study materials. Use the question bank for exam practice.

Curing and brining

Traditional methods include drying, curing, fermentation, smoking, pickling and preservation in fat.

Drying: jerky and biltong

Dry curing applies salt mixtures directly; brine curing uses a salt-containing liquid. Both require appropriate processing controls.

Smoking

Jerky and biltong are dried meat products; recipe, preparation and drying conditions vary.

Rillettes and fat preservation

Rillettes are slowly cooked meat packed under fat. Fat coverage reduces air contact but does not replace safe storage.

Fish sauce and sausage

Fish sauce fermentation breaks proteins into peptides and amino acids. Histamine control, hygiene, salt, temperature and storage are important in fish and meat products.
