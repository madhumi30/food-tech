Pickling

Concise notes prepared from the main concepts identified in your uploaded study materials. Use the question bank for exam practice.

Fermented and unfermented pickles

Pickling preserves foods using salt, acid or controlled fermentation.

Salt-stock pickling

Fermented pickles depend on microbial activity, often lactic acid production. Unfermented pickles rely mainly on added acid, salt or other controlled conditions.

Vinegar pickling

Salt affects water availability and microbial selection; vinegar supplies acetic acid and lowers pH.

Microbial diversity

Lactic acid bacteria are important in many fermented pickles; yeasts may also occur and can affect flavour or cause spoilage.

Safety

Suitable raw materials, hygiene, salt/acidity control and storage are important for product quality and safety.
