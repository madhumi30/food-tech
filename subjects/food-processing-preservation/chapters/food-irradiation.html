Food Irradiation

Concise notes prepared from the main concepts identified in your uploaded study materials. Use the question bank for exam practice.

Definition and principle

Food irradiation exposes food to a controlled dose of ionizing radiation to reduce selected microbial hazards, control insects or inhibit sprouting.

Radiation sources

The principal sources are gamma rays, electron beams and X-rays. Dose is measured in Gy or kGy.

Mechanism of preservation

Radiation can damage microbial genetic material directly or through reactive species formed in food.

Applications

Applications include spice decontamination, insect control in cereals and pulses, sprout inhibition in potatoes and selected pathogen or parasite control.

Advantages, limitations and safety

Approved irradiation does not make food radioactive. It cannot restore spoiled food or remove every toxin, and dose selection must be validated.
