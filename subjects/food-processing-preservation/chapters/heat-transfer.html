Modes of Heat Transfer

Concise notes prepared from the main concepts identified in your uploaded study materials. Use the question bank for exam practice.

Conduction

Conduction transfers heat through a material; convection transfers heat between a surface and a moving fluid; radiation transfers energy through electromagnetic waves.

Convection

Fourier’s law: q = −kA(dT/dx), where k is thermal conductivity and A is area.

Radiation

Convective heat transfer can be expressed as q = hA(Tf − Ts), where h is the convective heat-transfer coefficient.

Core equations

Natural convection is driven by buoyancy; forced convection uses a fan, pump or similar device.

Food-processing examples

Baking, boiling, grilling and drying can involve more than one mode of heat transfer.
